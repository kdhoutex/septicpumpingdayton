How much does septic pumping cost in Dayton?

It depends on tank size, access, and how full the tank is. Your quote is confirmed by the operator before work, with those factors stated up front.

What should I do if my aerobic alarm is on?

Cut water use, do not open the tank yourself, and request service. Note whether the red light, buzzer, or both are on, it helps diagnosis.
