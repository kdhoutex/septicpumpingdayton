Write down the visible symptoms

Record the alarm light or message, whether the issue is continuous, when it started, and whether household drains are affected. Use observations you can make safely from outside the equipment. Do not open a tank or touch electrical parts to complete a request.

Ask for diagnosis before replacement

Ask the provider to explain the fault, the proposed repair, the price of the diagnostic visit, and whether a replacement is actually needed. Request separate parts and labor pricing and the terms of any warranty.

If service is urgent

This site is not an emergency dispatch service. For a backup, overflow, or urgent alarm, contact an available septic provider directly. Do not wait for a response to an online referral request. Keep people and pets away from exposed sewage and follow professional guidance.

Pumping and pump replacement are different

Tank pumping removes contents. Pump replacement is equipment work. Tell the provider whether you need routine pumping or help with a mechanical or alarm problem so the correct person and equipment can be arranged.
