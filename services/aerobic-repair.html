Aerobic septic service and repair in Dayton, TX

Routine maintenance, tank pumping, and equipment repair are different tasks. Describe the problem before agreeing to a service.

Start with the existing service record

Check your system paperwork for the model, recent service visits, and the maintenance provider’s contact information. If you already have a maintenance arrangement, ask that provider what is covered before paying for a separate visit. In Liberty County, the county order requires a licensed maintenance contract for the life of an aerobic system; see our Liberty County septic rules guide.

Give useful observations, not a diagnosis

Note when the problem began, which alarm or indicator is showing, and any recent service or power interruption. You can report what you observe without deciding whether an aerator, float, pump, or controller has failed.

Check the right credentials

Texas has distinct credentials for aerobic maintenance and installation work. Ask who will perform the work and verify the credential relevant to that scope using TCEQ’s occupational licensing information. A pumping business and an aerobic maintenance provider are not interchangeable roles.

How often an aerobic system is checked

The EPA says systems with electrical float switches, pumps, or mechanical parts should be inspected more often than a conventional tank, generally once a year, and that a service contract matters because these systems have mechanized parts (EPA care guide). Liberty County goes further. Its order sets maintenance for homes at 4 visits per year, and the reports go to the county. Statewide, TCEQ says a homeowner may take over maintenance two years after installation unless the local authority has stricter rules (TCEQ maintenance page). Liberty County is one of the stricter ones.

Pumping an aerobic tank is a separate decision

A maintenance visit inspects and reports. It does not empty the tank. TCEQ says aerobic tanks are pumped based on sludge depth and the manufacturer's recommendations, so ask your maintenance provider for the sludge reading from the last report before you book a pump-out. See septic tank pumping for what to ask the pumping company.

What a Liberty County contract must show

The county order says a maintenance contract must show the permit number, the maintenance provider's license, the start and end dates, and the provider's phone and emergency phone. If an alarm sounds, the emergency phone on that contract is the first number to call. Crosby and Huffman are in Harris County, where the Liberty County order does not apply.

Before authorizing repair

Ask what the diagnostic visit costs, whether parts and labor are separate, what work your existing arrangement covers, and what documentation will be provided. This referral project does not diagnose equipment or guarantee a provider is available.
