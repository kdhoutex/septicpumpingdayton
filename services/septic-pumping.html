What to ask before booking

  • Which tanks or compartments are included, and how is the tank size established?
  • Are lid access, digging, disposal, and travel included or charged separately?
  • What happens if the operator finds an access or system problem?
  • Will you receive a written description of the work and a receipt?

Plan access without opening the tank

Tell the provider about gates, parking, a narrow driveway, pets, and known tank locations. Ask about hose reach and truck access. Do not assume a pumping truck can use the same route as a car. Let the professional decide how lids should be accessed.

How often is pumping needed?

The EPA describes three to five years as a typical household pumping interval, with actual needs depending on the tank, household, and use. Treat this as general guidance rather than a diagnosis or a schedule for every system. See the EPA maintenance guide.
